## Enumeration Literals
| Name          | **Description** |
|---------------|-----------------|
| MetricThread | The hole provides a metric thread for fixation. |
| ImperialThread | The hole provides an imperial thread for fixation. |
| Plain | The hole itself is plain and provides no specific features. |
| Through | The hole is in some kind metal sheet or similar. The fixing is stuck through the hole and expands in some way on the other side to reach a fixation. |

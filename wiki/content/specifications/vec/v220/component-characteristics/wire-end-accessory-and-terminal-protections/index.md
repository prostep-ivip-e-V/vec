<p> <i>WireEndAccessorySpecifications </i>are describing parts that are used for a treatment of a wire end before the actual terminal is attached to the <i>WireEnd</i> (e.g. a ferrule). In contrast to this a <i>TerminalProtection</i> is something that is applied after the joining process.      </p>
